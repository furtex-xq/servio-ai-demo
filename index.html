Why Servio

A busy service is four separate jobs that don't talk to each other.

Taking orders, cooking them, serving them and counting the money usually happen on paper, in someone's memory and in a till that knows nothing about the kitchen.

Guests wait

To get a menu, to order, to ask what's in a dish, to get the bill. Every wait is a table that turns slower.

Orders get lost

A ticket misread, an allergy not passed on, a dish forgotten on the pass. The kitchen only knows what reached it.

The owner is guessing

What sells, what sits on the menu doing nothing, which day is busiest — known at the end of the month, if at all.

The solution

One app, four screens, the same live data.

Every device in the restaurant runs Servio AI. Each one opens in the role it was given — and they all see the same orders the moment they change.

On the table

Guest tablet

Menu with photos and allergens, an AI waiter, cart, order status, a button to call the waiter.

In the kitchen

Kitchen display

New orders by station, timers that turn red when something is late, one tap to mark it ready.

In the waiter's pocket

Waiter phone

Tables, orders ready to serve, and every call from a guest — with the table number.

For the owner

Owner dashboard

Revenue, top dishes, menu, floor plan, team, devices and reservations — from anywhere.

Built for real service

The details that decide a Friday night.

Keeps working offline

Every device keeps its own copy of the menu and orders. If the internet drops, service goes on; it syncs when the connection is back.

Nine languages

English, German, Italian, French, Spanish, Polish, Czech, Ukrainian and Russian. A tourist switches the tablet to their own language in one tap.

An AI waiter that knows the menu

Guests ask what's vegetarian, what's spicy, what goes with a burger — and get answers from this restaurant's own menu, not from the internet.

Connect a device with a code

The owner creates a code; a new tablet types it in and becomes a guest tablet, a kitchen screen or a waiter phone. No IT person needed.

Each restaurant sees only its own data

Access is checked on the server for every request, not just hidden in the app. One restaurant can never read another's orders.

Runs on ordinary Android tablets

No proprietary terminals. Any Android tablet or phone the restaurant already has, or buys off the shelf.
